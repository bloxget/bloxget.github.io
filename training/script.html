Training Script

Copy and use these commands during training sessions. everything is formatted for quick access and smooth moderation.

start of training

greeting message
welcome to bloxget training session please line up on the purple lines and wait for instructions from staff
session rules
before we begin, please remember: - no trolling - no exploiting - respect all staff members - failure to comply may result in removal

during training

line up instruction
please line up neatly on the purple lines do not jump or move out of formation
attention command
attention all trainees you are now required to listen carefully to all instructions

end of training

closing message
thank you for attending today’s training you may now leave the training area
