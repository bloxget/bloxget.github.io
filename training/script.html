Find answers to common training questions, commands, and system explanations used during BloxGet sessions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the purple lines used for?

The purple lines are used to organize trainees during sessions. They ensure proper spacing, structure, and order throughout training.

Training System
Where can I find trainer commands?

All trainer commands are available inside the Trainer Script section. These include session controls such as start, freeze, and reset tools.

Commands
What are the training rules?

Training rules are guidelines that all participants must follow during sessions. Failure to follow them may result in warnings or removal from the session.

Rules
How does the rank system work?

The rank system determines progression inside BloxGet. Players improve their rank by attending trainings, showing activity, and performing well during sessions.

Progression
What should I do if I need help during a session?

If you need assistance during a training session, you should ask a staff member in-game or contact support through the official Discord server.
