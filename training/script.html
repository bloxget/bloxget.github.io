Training Script

Copy and use these commands during training sessions. Everything is formatted for quick access.

Start of training

Greeting message
Welcome to a BloxGet training session! Please line up on the purple lines and wait for instructions from staff
Session rules
Before we begin, please remember: - no trolling - no exploiting - respect all staff members - failure to comply may result in removal

During training

Line up instruction
Please line up neatly on the purple lines. Do not jump or otherwise move out of formation
Attention command
Attention all trainees. You are required to listen carefully to all instructions.

End of training

closing message
Thank you for attending today’s training, and congrats on making it. Please line up at the pink lines to get ranked.
